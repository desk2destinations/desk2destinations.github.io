# Instagram Reels Workflow — Desk2Destinations

A repeatable system for turning blog posts into Reels that grow @desk2destinations_ on Instagram. Built for a 2-person team with day jobs — minimal editing time, maximum reuse of content already on the site.

---

## 1. The growth thesis

Instagram in 2026 rewards 3 things, in this order:
1. **Watch time / completion rate** — Reels under 15s with a strong hook outperform longer ones.
2. **Saves & shares** — practical, "I'll need this later" content beats pretty-but-passive content.
3. **Replies in DMs** — captions ending with a question that's easy to answer get pushed to more feeds.

Translation for our content:
- Lead every Reel with a **bold first 1.5s** — text overlay + visual change.
- Build at least 1 Reel per week that's a **saveable list** (e.g. "5 things to do in Hiroshima in one day").
- End every caption with a **question** ("Is okonomiyaki worth the queue?", "Tokyo or Kyoto for first-timers?").

Target cadence: **3 Reels/week** (Mon, Wed, Sat) + **2 carousels/week** (Tue, Fri) + **1 story sequence/day**. If that's too much, halve it but keep the Sat Reel — weekends drive 40%+ of weekly reach.

## 2. The 5-step weekly workflow

**Sunday — 30 minutes — Trend scan**
1. Open Instagram Reels. Scroll your Reels feed for 10 min. Save any Reel where the *audio* has a rising arrow icon (trending).
2. Check `Creator Studio → Reels → Trending Audio` (or use the third-party tool **Mojo** / **InShot**'s trend list).
3. Check what travel creators are posting this week: @mythicalnomads, @travel.couple, @somewhereonearth_, @anushka.thatihaveseenfromahabit.
4. Save 5 trending audios into a folder called "This week's hooks" in your IG Saved Collections.

**Sunday — 30 minutes — Content matchmaking**
1. Open the **"Reel scripts" bank below** (section 4).
2. Pick 3 scripts whose mood matches the 5 trending audios from step 4.
3. For each, note: blog post source, B-roll clips needed, voiceover/captions, audio.

**Monday/Wednesday/Saturday — 30 min per Reel — Shoot or assemble**
1. Open the blog post in this site → grab existing photos/videos.
2. If footage is missing: shoot 4-5 short verticals on phone (1080×1920, 24-30 fps, locked exposure).
3. Edit in **CapCut** or **InShot** (both free) using the saved trending audio.
4. Captions: use the per-Reel template in section 5.

**Tuesday/Friday — 20 min — Carousel from blog**
1. Pick one blog post.
2. Build a 5-10 slide carousel in **Canva** (templates: "Travel Itinerary", "Day in the Life").
3. Slide 1 = hook, slides 2-N = list/itinerary, last slide = CTA ("Full guide at desk2destinations.com").

**Friday evening — 15 min — Analytics review**
- Check Instagram Insights → Reels → last 7 days.
- For each Reel: % completion, saves, sends, follows from reel.
- Note the **single Reel with the highest save rate**. Make another like it next week. That's the entire iteration loop.

## 4. Reel script bank — 12 ready to shoot from existing posts

Each script lists: hook (first 3s), middle (visuals), end (CTA), source blog post.

### 4.1 "The bus that left 3 minutes early" (Hiroshima)
- **Hook:** Text overlay over a shot of a bus stop: *"In Japan, the overnight bus left 3 minutes EARLY."*
- **Visual:** Quick cuts — clock, bus pulling out, sleeping passenger, sunrise over Tokyo, you stretching in Shinjuku.
- **End:** *"This is why Japan works." → "Full Hiroshima diary in bio."*
- **Audio:** Calm/serene trending sound, or "Tokyo Drift" instrumental.
- **Source:** japan-hiroshima.html
- **Why it works:** Specific number in hook = curiosity gap. Universal "Japan punctuality" theme = saveable.

### 4.2 "I ate at this Kyoto ramen shop and now I have a problem" (Kyoto Engine)
- **Hook:** Close-up steaming bowl of chilli ramen + text: *"I haven't stopped craving this since."*
- **Visual:** The bowl, the chopstick lift, your face on first bite, the queue outside, the receipt.
- **End:** *"Kyoto Engine. Veg chilli ramen. That's the post."*
- **Audio:** "Show me what you got" or a trending food-reaction audio.
- **Source:** japan-kyoto.html → Kyoto Engine section.
- **Why it works:** Food reaction Reels have the highest save rate in travel.

### 4.3 "The Lawson Incident" (Tokyo)
- **Hook:** Text: *"Storytime: how Japan's convenience store culture broke us."*
- **Visual:** Lawson storefront → shelves → the moment (act it out lightly) → final realisation.
- **End:** *"Have you had a Lawson moment? 👇"*
- **Audio:** Trending storytime audio (slow build).
- **Source:** japan-tokyo.html → Lawson Incident section.
- **Why it works:** Storytime format + relatable travel-fail = high comment rate.

### 4.4 "Tadao Ando hid a giant Buddha inside a hill" (Sapporo)
- **Hook:** Walking through the entrance tunnel, text: *"What's at the end of this tunnel will give you chills."*
- **Visual:** POV tunnel walk → reveal of Buddha statue → wide shot with lavender field → architectural details.
- **End:** *"Hill of the Buddha, Sapporo. Save this."*
- **Audio:** Cinematic ambient.
- **Source:** japan-sapporo.html → Hill of the Buddha.
- **Why it works:** Tunnel reveal = perfect visual hook. Architecture content over-indexes on saves.

### 4.5 "Hokkaido is a different country" (Sapporo)

## 10. The 4 mistakes to avoid

1. **Don't post without an audio** — IG suppresses silent/original-audio Reels for new accounts under 10K followers.
2. **Don't use 30 hashtags** — IG officially recommends 3-5. We use 13 (mix of sizes) and that's the upper limit.
3. **Don't post and ghost** — reply to comments within the first hour. The algorithm watches engagement *velocity*.
4. **Don't chase virality on Reel #1** — first 10 Reels are about training the algorithm on your niche. Stay consistent (couple + travel + honest) and metrics will compound.
