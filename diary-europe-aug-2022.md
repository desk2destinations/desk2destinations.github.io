Day 2: Maggie in host's microwave, skydive cancelled, day trip to Grindelwald (rainy, cloudy, gloomy), cards declined nightmare (3 cards failed, only SBI debit card worked at COOP, cafe owner kind), Panoramic View train to Gstaad → onwards to Montreux. Observation: "Interlaken is way better than Zurich & Lucerne", Jungfrau dodged b/c weather (saved money — top of Europe + most costly).

**Montreux (19 Aug, ~2 hours stopover):** Final meal in Europe — pizza & drinks; train back to Zurich same night. Just one hour before Geneva; chose not to visit Geneva.

**Trip totals:** ~15 days, 2 people, ₹6.80 lakh budget. Return via Warsaw airport (beetroot burger). Diary completed 3 Oct 2022 in Mumbai.
