Japan

16 picks
Mercy Vegan Cafe
Osaka — near Shinsaibashi

VeganGenerous bowls and burgers — the rare full-vegan spot in central Osaka.

Rikuro's Jiggly Cheesecake
Osaka — Daimaru basement

DessertCatch it warm out of the oven, watch it wobble, eat it on the steps outside.

Hokkaido Soft Serve (Dairy-Free)
Sapporo — Pole Town arcade

VeganThe dairy-free option from the corner stand that hits in any season.

Tofu Set Meals near Nanzen-ji
Kyoto

VegetarianThe temple-side restaurants serve yudofu set menus that taste like a quiet morning.

Konbini Onigiri (Umeboshi / Konbu)
Everywhere in Japan

Vegetarian7-Eleven, Lawson, FamilyMart — the engineering on the wrapper alone is worth the trip.

Matcha Castella from Nakamura Tokichi
Kyoto — Uji

DessertTake the train to Uji. Drink the tea. Take the cake home.

Kyoto Engine — Veg Chilli Ramen
Kyoto

VeganTiny counter, ferocious chilli oil — sit at the bar and watch them work.

Vegan Izakaya — Gyoza & Sake
Kyoto

VeganOrder the gyoza, order the cold sake flight, do not look at the clock.

Ain Soph
Tokyo (Ginza) & Kyoto

VeganWorth-the-queue pancakes and burgers — Ginza branch for the soufflé stack.

T's TanTan
Tokyo Station

VeganInside the ticket gates — tantanmen for transit days, no platform left behind.

Fujifuku
Tokyo — Asakusa

VeganQuiet basement set menus — the kind of place locals book a month out.

Komeda Café (Plant-Based Kissa)
Tokyo

VeganAn old-school kissaten that quietly added a full plant-based menu. Order the morning toast set.

Afuri Ramen
Tokyo — Akihabara

VegetarianYuzu-shio broth that tastes like sunshine. Ask for the vegetarian version.

MACCHA HOUSE — Matcha Parfait
Kyoto

DessertThe layered parfait — mochi, jelly, matcha ice cream, more matcha. We split one, then ordered another.

Hatoya Matcha & The Matcha House (Asakusa)
Tokyo — Asakusa

VeganTwo stalls on the same lane, both worth it — sample, then commit.

2foods Vegan Café
Tokyo — Ginza

VegetarianBright, fast, plant-based — the burgers travel well to a park bench.

Spain

8 picks
Patatas Bravas (done right)
Barcelona — El Born

VegetarianThe smoky-paprika sauce is the whole point — skip the place if they hand you ketchup.

Teresa Carles
Barcelona — Eixample

VeganThe grown-up vegan dinner spot — book ahead, order the seasonal tasting plates.

Churros con Chocolate
Madrid — Chocolatería San Ginés

DessertOpen since 1894. Go after midnight, order a plate of six, dunk slowly.

La Campana
Seville — old town

VegetarianThe 19th-century pastry counter where the locals start their day. Coffee + tocino de cielo.

Vegan Bowl & Arepas
Seville

VeganThe Latin-American detour that saved us mid-trip — black beans, plantain, mango salsa.

Artisanal Thin-Crust Pizza
Seville — backstreet pizzeria

VegetarianOff the cathedral square — the kind of place with three tables and one excellent oven.

La Boqueria Market
Barcelona — Las Ramblas

VegetarianSkip the front-row tourist stalls, walk to the back — fresh juice, olives, almonds.

Backstreet Churros
Barcelona — off El Born

DessertThe no-name corner cart that locals queue at on Sunday mornings.

Italy

8 picks
Pizza Margherita (Naples-style)
Rome — Trastevere wood-fire spots

VegetarianSoft, wet centre, leopard-spotted crust. Eat with a knife and fork — it is fine.

Cacio e Pepe
Rome — any honest trattoria

VegetarianThree ingredients, infinite ways to ruin it. Eat it in Rome and never eat it anywhere else.

Granita di Limone
Amalfi Coast — beachfront stalls

VeganCrushed ice and lemons from the cliff terraces. The only correct way to survive a coast walk.

Gustapizza
Florence — Oltrarno

VegetarianHeart-shaped pies on request, queue out the door — go at opening, sit by the river.

Romeo Gelateria
Rome

VegetarianPistachio so green it argues with the cone. Get the small cup and add the second flavour.

Tiramisu at Two Sizes
Rome — Trastevere

DessertThe original takeaway tiramisu cup — pistachio version is the dark-horse winner.

I Love Italy — Fresh Pasta
Venice

VegetarianTakeaway pasta from a window — perfect for an afternoon on a quiet canal step.

Veg Cuoppo d'Amalfi
Amalfi — seafront

VegetarianPaper cone of fried zucchini, courgette flowers, polenta chips. Salt, lemon, sea air.

Germany

7 picks
Käsespätzle
Stuttgart / Black Forest

VegetarianEgg noodles, mountain cheese, crispy onions. The German answer to mac & cheese.

Curry 36 (Veggie Currywurst)
Berlin — Mehringdamm

VeganThe veggie option is real and good — eat it standing up at 2 a.m., as intended.

Black Forest Cake
Black Forest — traditional café

DessertThe real Schwarzwälder Kirschtorte uses kirsch, not vanilla — refuse the imitations.

Markthalle Pretzels
Stuttgart — Markthalle

VegetarianCrusted with rock salt, butter melting on the hot soft side. Best breakfast on the go.

English Garden Biergarten
Munich — English Garden

VegetarianPretzels, obatzda cheese, radishes, a litre of helles — picnic under the chestnut trees.

Triberg Black Forest Cake
Triberg — Café Schäfer

DessertThe café that holds the original 1934 recipe. Order it with a small black coffee.

Lake Titisee Ice Cream
Titisee — lakefront

VegetarianHazelnut + cherry, two scoops, eaten with feet hanging over the dock.

Pastel de Nata

DessertWarm, scorched on top, custard still wobbling. Sprinkle cinnamon. Repeat.

France

3 picks
Marais Falafel
Paris — Le Marais

VegetarianL'As du Fallafel is the famous one — and worth the queue. Take it to Place des Vosges.

Early-Morning Croissants
Paris — neighbourhood boulangerie

VegetarianFind the bakery with the longest local queue at 7:30 a.m. — that one. Eat warm.

Kugelhopf

DessertThe fluted Alsatian brioche with almonds — looks like a crown, eats like Sunday.

New Zealand

5 picks
Pizza by the Penguin Colony
South Island — Oamaru

VegetarianWood-fired margherita ten minutes from where the little blue penguins waddle in at dusk.

Patagonia Chocolates & Ice Cream
Queenstown — lakefront

VegetarianArgentinian-style hot chocolate. Order a small cup, sit by the lake, do nothing.

Mrs Ferg Gelato + Cookie
Queenstown

Glenorchy Café
